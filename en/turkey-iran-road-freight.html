Turkey–Iran road freight management with Yoldash

Cross-border road freight between Turkey and Iran depends on clear routing, cargo details, the right vehicle, timing and documentation. Yoldash is built to make coordination between transport participants more structured and transparent.

Yoldash App / WebLoad board

Route and cargo details

Origin, destination and the essential cargo information are presented early in the workflow so opportunities can be assessed faster.

Driver and vehicle assignment

Driver and vehicle information can be managed as part of the shipment assignment process.

Offers and counter-offers

A structured offer flow helps reduce fragmented negotiation across multiple channels.

Shipment workflow

The target Yoldash lifecycle includes load creation, publishing, offer, counter-offer, acceptance, driver and vehicle assignment, private chat, loading, in-transit, delivery and completion.

Documents and operations

Documents, CMR, trip expenses and Turkey insurance workflows are among the operational tools in the Yoldash product scope. Official requirements still need to be checked for each cargo and route.

Frequently asked questions

How can I find a carrier for Turkey–Iran freight?

A shipper can publish the route and core cargo information in Yoldash so relevant transport users can review the opportunity and submit an offer.

Is Yoldash itself a freight carrier?

Yoldash is a digital marketplace and shipment-management platform. The contractual role of each participant should be defined for each shipment.

Does Yoldash support CMR workflows?

CMR and document management are part of the operational feature set planned and implemented within the Yoldash ecosystem.
