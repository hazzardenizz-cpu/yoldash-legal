Curtainsider road freight

Curtainsider trailers are widely used for general freight, but suitability depends on cargo dimensions, weight, loading method and protection requirements.

Cargo suitability

Many palletized and general cargo types can use curtainsiders, subject to actual shipment requirements.

Side or top loading

If special loading access is required, state it clearly in the load listing.

Dimensions and weight

Cargo dimensions, weight and loading layout matter for vehicle planning.

Clear Yoldash listing

Specify trailer type, truck count, loading time and special conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Is a curtainsider suitable for every load?

No. Cargo characteristics and loading method must be checked.

Can I specify loading method in the listing?

Yes. Clear requirements help carriers assess the shipment correctly.
