Turkey–Iran container road freight

Container road transport between Turkey and Iran requires clear container specifications, compatible chassis and tractor setup, cargo weight, pickup and delivery points, customs planning and realistic timing.

Container specifications

Record the container type and size, whether it is loaded or empty, gross weight, seal information when applicable and any handling restrictions. These details reduce mismatches between the cargo requirement and the vehicle dispatched to the pickup point.

Vehicle and chassis compatibility

The tractor and chassis configuration should match the container and route requirements. Equipment selection should also account for legal weight limits, terminal procedures and the conditions at the loading and delivery locations.

Pickup, border and delivery planning

State the terminal, warehouse or delivery point precisely. For Turkey–Iran movements, border timing and required transport documents should be prepared before dispatch. Review the Turkey–Iran border freight guide and road freight documents before planning the movement.

Load-listing details

A useful load listing includes container type, weight, origin, destination, loading date, truck count, contact requirements and special operating conditions. Clear listings help carriers and drivers understand the job before making an offer.

Insurance and CMR

Container transport should be planned together with the relevant CMR information and cargo insurance requirements. See the CMR guide and Turkey freight insurance guide for related checks.
