Frequently asked questions

What is Yoldash?

Yoldash is a digital road-freight platform connecting cargo owners, drivers and transport companies.

Can guests use Yoldash?

Some parts of the platform can be viewed as a guest, while actions such as submitting offers or managing shipments may require an account and a completed profile.

Which languages are available?

The web experience supports Persian, Turkish and English.
