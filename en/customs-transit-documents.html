Customs and transit documents: TIR, NCTS and HS

Organize shipment records around the actual customs procedure and verify specialist transit information.

Identify the procedure first

Export, import and transit are different procedures. Determine the relevant offices and responsible declaration party before making a document checklist. Compare invoice, packing list, transport note, vehicle information and any product-specific records requested for the actual shipment.

TIR and transit controls

TIR is a customs transit and guarantee system involving approved sealed load compartments and customs controls. It is not a substitute for the cargo description or commercial invoice. Confirm the procedure, seal details and completion records with the responsible operator and customs party.

NCTS and reference numbers

For a journey using NCTS, verify the declaration reference and accompanying information through the responsible declarant. Do not treat a reference number alone as proof that all steps have completed. Keep the documents and status confirmations together and use the competent authority’s current guidance.

HS classification

Do not guess a classification from a short product name. Supply composition, use and technical details to the qualified person making the classification. Confirm the national tariff requirements for the specific goods and country; this guide does not assign a customs code.
