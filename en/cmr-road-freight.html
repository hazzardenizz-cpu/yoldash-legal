CMR in Turkey–Iran road freight

CMR is a widely recognized document in international road transport. It is commonly used to record core information about the sender, consignee, carrier, cargo and route.

What information can a CMR contain?

Depending on the shipment, it may include shipper and consignee details, place of delivery, cargo description, package count, weight, carrier information and transport notes.

Why consistency matters

Key shipment facts should remain consistent across the load listing, operational records and transport documents. Material differences should be reviewed before departure.

CMR management in Yoldash

Yoldash aims to keep CMR-related information alongside the broader shipment workflow so participants can work from a more structured operational record.

Pre-departure checklist

  • Sender and consignee
  • Origin and destination
  • Cargo description and quantity
  • Weight or relevant measurements
  • Vehicle details when required
  • Special notes or reservations
