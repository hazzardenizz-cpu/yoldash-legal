Turkey–Iran load board for shippers and drivers

The Yoldash load board is designed to make the essential details of each freight opportunity clear from the start, helping drivers and carriers assess relevant loads without relying on incomplete messages.

Clear load listings

Origin, destination, listing party, required vehicle count, publication time and access to details are treated as core listing information.

Carrier offers

Drivers and carriers can submit transport offers based on the load conditions, while shippers can review them in one workflow.

Driver and vehicle marketplace

Yoldash also supports marketplace use cases around finding drivers or vehicles, not only publishing cargo.

Why use a digital load board?

A structured load board reduces the fragmentation of cargo information across calls, groups and messaging apps, making opportunities easier to compare.

What should a load listing include?

  • Origin and destination
  • Cargo type and description
  • Required vehicle or trailer type
  • Number of vehicles
  • Loading date or cargo-ready time
  • Price or negotiation conditions when applicable

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to publish a load?

Publishing and managing offers may require an account and the profile information required by the platform.

How do drivers find loads?

Published cargo opportunities appear on the load board, where drivers can review relevant route and cargo information.

Can I use Yoldash on mobile?

The web experience is responsive, and the Yoldash app is part of the same broader service ecosystem.
