Turkey–Iran road freight route and border planning

A Turkey–Iran road freight route involves more than driving distance. Border planning, documentation, vehicle readiness, timing and delivery coordination all matter. Gürbulak–Bazargan is one of the best-known road connections between the two countries.

Gürbulak and Bazargan

Gürbulak is on the Turkish side and Bazargan is on the Iranian side of a major road connection between the two countries. The appropriate route for a shipment should be chosen using current operating conditions.

Planning time

Cargo-ready time, driver arrival, potential waiting time and delivery schedules should be considered in the transport plan.

Documents and cargo information

Shipment records should be consistent with the actual cargo. Current customs and border requirements should be verified using official or authorized sources.
