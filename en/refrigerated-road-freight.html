No. Temperature, volume, loading method and operating conditions must be checked.

Information to confirm before booking a refrigerated vehicle

Prepare the product name, gross weight, package count, dimensions and readiness window. Obtain the required temperature range from the product specification and the shipper’s instructions rather than choosing a generic setting. Confirm the condition and temperature of the goods when handed over for carriage.

  • Temperature range and the agreed method of monitoring and recording.
  • Packaging, airflow requirements and stacking restrictions.
  • Warehouse access and equipment for loading and unloading.
  • The contact and process for reporting delays or a temperature deviation.

Handover checklist for sensitive cargo

Confirm vehicle suitability and cleanliness before loading. At delivery, compare package count, visible condition, time and available monitoring records with the receiver. Record discrepancies clearly and follow the agreed escalation process. A refrigerated vehicle label alone does not establish that the equipment can meet the needs of a specific product.
