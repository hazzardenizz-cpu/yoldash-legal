Yoldash guide sources and content approach

How Yoldash prepares educational freight guides, cites sources, labels illustrative figures and describes content update dates.

How the guides are prepared

Yoldash guides explain freight terminology, help users prepare shipment information and provide practical checklists. AI tools may assist with drafting. Publication on this website is not an official opinion from a customs authority, bank, insurer or legal adviser.

Sources and specialist rules

When a guide discusses a specialist rule, check the source linked within that article. CMR material can be followed through UNECE, Incoterms rules through ICC and TIR explanations through IRU. Use the official text and the circumstances of the actual shipment when making operational decisions.

Illustrative figures and dates

Figures in examples illustrate a method rather than a current market rate or a binding freight offer. A stated update date identifies a content revision of that page; it does not guarantee that every regulation or border condition is current. Reconfirm route and pricing information before carriage.

Report a correction

Send the page URL, the passage in question and a supporting correction source to info@getyoldash.com. Do not include unnecessary identity documents or bank details.
