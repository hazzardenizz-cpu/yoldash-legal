Insurance information for road freight in Turkey

Insurance requirements in international road freight can vary by vehicle, cargo, carrier responsibility, contract and route. Yoldash includes an operational workflow for recording and organizing information related to Turkey insurance processes.

Information to keep ready

Driver and vehicle details, transit plates, policy information, validity dates and any documents required for the journey should be reviewed before departure.

Vehicle, liability and cargo cover

Vehicle insurance, carrier liability and cargo insurance are different concepts. The required cover should be determined from the transport contract and applicable official requirements.

Why record it in Yoldash?

Keeping related information in one workflow can reduce document fragmentation and make pre-trip checks easier for transport participants.

Important limitation

Yoldash is not an insurer or legal authority. Required coverage and policy validity should be confirmed with an authorized insurer, carrier or relevant official source.

Frequently asked questions

Does Yoldash sell insurance?

No. Yoldash is a transport-management platform; the insurance workflow is for organizing information related to operations.

Is one policy enough for every shipment?

Not necessarily. Cargo type, vehicle, contractual responsibilities and route can change the coverage required.

When should insurance details be checked?

Validity and required documents should be checked before the journey and before relevant border procedures.
